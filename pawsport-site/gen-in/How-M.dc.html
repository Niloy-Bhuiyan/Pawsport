Straight talk

What we never do. And what we always do.

Never

We never

  • Give veterinary advice. Your vet decides what your pet needs.
  • Give legal advice or interpret the law for you.
  • Promise approval at the border. Nobody can.
  • Text, email or call you without your yes.

We always

  • Link every rule to an official source.
  • Have a person check changes before you are told.
  • Say clearly that rules can change.
  • Give you a way to opt out of every message.
  • Put a human on the line when you ask.
